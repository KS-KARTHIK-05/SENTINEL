\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

We studied how a UAV swarm without infrastructure can get victim reports to responders in time.
SENTINEL lets UAVs search freely, triggers delivery from the information each UAV has confirmed,
fuses views so that occluded angles cannot veto a clear detection, and preserves the links of a
delivery path for every feasible step (Proposition~\ref{prop:link}). In about 11\,000 simulated missions against seven
tuned baselines, it reported significantly more victims within 60\,s than every baseline in most
settings (urban, suburban and open channels, all failure models in urban rubble, swarms of 3--50 UAVs
on the 400\,m area, moving obstacles and detector uncertainty); in the severe channel it was ahead
of or tied with the best baseline, and it was never significantly worse. A PX4/Gazebo cross-check
agreed with the fast simulator within 0.07.
The ablation located the gains in the delivery trigger and the fusion rule rather than in
continuous connectivity. Future work will adapt the delivery trigger to the observed contact rate,
calibrate the channel model with measurements, and validate the system in hardware flights.
