\begin{abstract}
\begin{revised}
UAV swarms searching a disaster area without infrastructure must carry victim reports to the
responders over their own radio links. SENTINEL treats the network as an elastic mesh that
stretches and breaks during search and is re-formed only to deliver a confirmed report: Elastic-Mesh
PSO (EM-PSO) plans waypoints, confirmed information triggers delivery, weak detections get a
second view, and an outage-based Signal-Spring keeps delivery links reliable. In about
\nMissions{} simulated missions with a \nFreq\,GHz outage channel, \nEoneNmin--\nEoneNmax{} UAVs, six
failure settings and seven tuned baselines, SENTINEL reports \nEthreeGainLo--\nEthreeGainHi\,\% more
victims within 60\,s than the best baseline in urban rubble; PX4 software-in-the-loop runs agree with the simulator, and an always-connected mesh
reports far fewer victims in time.
\end{revised}
\end{abstract}

\section{\kept{Introduction}}
\begin{revised}
After an earthquake, thermal-camera UAVs can sweep rubble and detect people on board
\cite{hituav,yolo26}. A detection matters only once responders receive it, over the UAVs'
short-range links, which buildings block, fading weakens and crashes cut. A connected swarm
delivers at once but searches little, a spread-out one finds more but delivers by chance; we measure
this discovery--delivery trade-off as the share of victims reported within 60\,s.

Our first version held each UAV within 50\,m of its nearest neighbour, but a fixed radius ignores
rubble, a close neighbour does not imply a connected network (Lemma~\ref{lem:nn}), and its 25\,m test
area was smaller than the radio range. This revision contributes (i)~\emph{information-triggered
delivery}: a UAV stops searching when a victim it confirmed has not reached the responder within a hold
time $T_h$; (ii)~\emph{corroborative sensing}: weak detections get a lateral second view, fused so that
a blocked angle cannot veto a clear one; and (iii)~\emph{EM-PSO with an outage-based Signal-Spring and
link preservation}: EM-PSO plans the waypoints throughout, while its spring and link preservation act
only during delivery, as a safety property that keeps preserved links reliable
(Proposition~\ref{prop:link}) and cuts link violations \nViolFold-fold; the reporting gains come from
(i) and (ii). Code, generated scenarios, per-seed results and extended tables are available
at \url{https://github.com/KS-KARTHIK-05/SENTINEL} (branch v2).
\end{revised}

\section{\kept{Related Work}}
\begin{revised}
Connectivity controllers keep the communication graph connected at all times, through its algebraic
connectivity $\lambda_2$ \cite{zavlanos2008,sabattini2013} or edge potentials \cite{ji2007}, and all assume a disk radio model. Intermittent connectivity schedules meetings
\cite{kantaros2017}, and age-of-information collection serves sources at known positions
\cite{bafarassat2026aoi}, whereas victims are found by the search itself. Distributed PSO and Bayesian
search \cite{yang2024dapso,niu2026eventbayes} assume communication when needed, and multi-agent
reinforcement learning such as MAPPO \cite{yu2022mappo} needs a trained policy. None combines
online-discovered sources, a deadline and an obstruction-aware channel.
\end{revised}

\section{System Model}\label{sec:model}
\begin{revised}
$N$ UAVs fly at altitude $h$ over tall no-fly, link-blocking buildings and low rubble that hides
victims; a responder radio at the launch point is the sink. Each slot $\Delta t$ every UAV
broadcasts its position, victim ledger and acknowledgements, received at $j$ with SINR
\begin{equation}
\gamma_{ij}=\frac{P_tG^2\,10^{-(\mathrm{PL}(d_{ij})+n_{ij}L_o+X_{ij})/10}\,|h_{ij}|^2}{\sigma^2+\sum_{k\in\mathcal{I}_j}\bar P_{kj}},\label{eq:sinr}
\end{equation}
with log-distance path loss $\mathrm{PL}(d)=\mathrm{PL}(d_0)+10\eta\log_{10}(d/d_0)$, $n_{ij}$
obstacles at $L_o$ each, correlated shadowing $X_{ij}$, Rician fading (Rayleigh when obstructed) and
colliding transmitters $\mathcal{I}_j$. Decoding needs $\gamma_{ij}\ge\gamma_{\mathrm{th}}$, so
\begin{equation}
P_{\mathrm{out}}(d,n)=\mathbb{E}_X\big[F_{|h|^2}\big(\gamma_{\mathrm{th}}/(\bar\gamma(d,n)\,10^{X/10})\big)\big],\label{eq:pout}
\end{equation}
and the reliable range is $R_\varepsilon(n)=\max\{d:P_{\mathrm{out}}(d,n)\le\varepsilon\}$. The link
budget ($\nFreq$\,GHz, $P_t=\nPtx$\,dBm, $G=\nGant$\,dBi, $B=\nBw$\,MHz, noise figure \nNF\,dB,
$\gamma_{\mathrm{th}}=\nGth$\,dB) leaves \nBudget\,dB of path loss before fading margins, and profile
parameters follow published ranges \cite{tr36777,itup1411}. In urban rubble a link is reliable to
\nRurbanZero\,m in the open and \nRurbanOne\,m behind one obstruction (Table~\ref{tab:channel}), so the
old 50\,m radius describes one obstructed link. With $\tau_k$ victim $k$'s first confirmation and
$T_k$ its delivery time at the responder ($\infty$ if never), the team maximises from local information
\begin{equation}
J=\frac{1}{|\mathcal{V}|}\sum_{k\in\mathcal{V}}e^{-T_k/\theta},\qquad\theta=\nTheta\,\mathrm{s}.\label{eq:J}
\end{equation}
\end{revised}

\section{SENTINEL}\label{sec:method}
\begin{revised}
\paragraph{Mode Switching.} With $\mathcal{E}_i(t)$ the confirmed, undelivered entries UAV $i$ owns
(its own, and any older than $3T_h$, so a crashed UAV's copies still arrive), it enters \textsc{Deliver} when
\begin{equation}
\max_{e\in\mathcal{E}_i(t)}(t-\tau_e)\ge T_h\label{eq:trigger}
\end{equation}
and returns to \textsc{Explore} when $\mathcal{E}_i(t)$ is empty.

\paragraph{EM-PSO.} Each planning period a UAV picks the reachable waypoint that maximises
\begin{equation}
F_i(x)=w_c\hat G_i(x)+w_d\!\!\sum_{e\in\mathcal{T}_i}\!c_e e^{-\frac{\|x-x_e\|^2}{2s^2}}
+\frac{w_g(\|x_i-g_i\|-\|x-g_i\|)-w_t\|x-x_i\|}{vT_p}-S_i(x),\label{eq:fitness}
\end{equation}
with $\hat G_i$ expected new detections, $\mathcal{T}_i$ the tentative entries, $g_i$ a frontier
goal and $w_t$ a travel cost. The Signal-Spring acts only while tethered (delivering or relaying):
\begin{equation}
S_i(x)=\lambda_i\sum_{j\in\mathcal{S}_i}\Big(\frac{[P_{\mathrm{out}}(\|x-x_j\|,n(x,x_j))-\varepsilon_i]_+}{\varepsilon_i}\Big)^2,\label{eq:spring}
\end{equation}
where $\lambda_i=\lambda_0(1+\beta U_i)$, $\varepsilon_i=\varepsilon/(1+\beta U_i)$ and $U_i$ counts
entries only $i$ holds; it penalises outage, not distance. A constriction PSO
($\chi=\nPsoChi$, $c_1=c_2=\nPsoC$) \cite{clerc2002} maximises $F_i$.

\paragraph{Link Preservation.} $\mathcal{S}_i$ holds $i$'s neighbours in the local minimum spanning
tree of reliable links \cite{li2003lmst}.
\begin{lemma}\label{lem:nn}
Nearest-neighbour distance constraints do not imply connectivity.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
UAVs at $(0,0)$, $(10,0)$, $(200,0)$, $(210,0)$ with $R=50$\,m each have a neighbour at 10\,m, yet no
link joins the pairs.
\end{proof}
While tethered, UAV $i$ scales its step $u_i$ by the largest $\alpha\in[0,1]$ such that
$x_i'=x_i+\alpha u_i$ satisfies, for all $j\in\mathcal{S}_i$,
\begin{equation}
\|x_i'-m_{ij}\|\le\tfrac12 r_{ij}-\tfrac12 v\,a_{ij},\qquad r_{ij}=R_\varepsilon\big(n^{\delta_{ij}}(x_i',\hat x_j)\big),\label{eq:proj}
\end{equation}
with $\hat x_j$ the last reported position of $j$ (age $a_{ij}$), $m_{ij}=(x_i+\hat x_j)/2$,
$\delta_{ij}=v(\Delta t+a_{ij})$ and $n^\delta$ counting obstacles inflated by $\delta$.
\begin{proposition}\label{prop:link}
If $j\in\mathcal{S}_i$, $i\in\mathcal{S}_j$, both UAVs are tethered, speeds are bounded by $v$,
positions are at most $a$ old and both steps satisfy Eq.~\eqref{eq:proj}, link $(i,j)$ is
$\varepsilon$-reliable at $t+\Delta t$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
Each midpoint estimate is within $va/2$ of the true one, so Eq.~\eqref{eq:proj} and the triangle
inequality give $\|x_i'-x_j'\|\le\frac12(r_{ij}+r_{ji})$; the inflated count bounds the true one, and
$R_\varepsilon$ is non-increasing in it.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Corroborative Sensing.} An entry with $\nClo\le c_e<\nChi$ asks the nearest neighbour for a
view from $\pm\nFlank$\,m perpendicular to the line of sight, which at $h=\nAlt$\,m looks
\nFlankAngle$^\circ$ off nadir past a slab edge at only \nFlankSlant\,\% more slant range. Keeping the
best confidence $c_{e,v}$ per viewing sector $v$, SENTINEL fuses
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{logit}\,c_e=\max_v\mathrm{logit}\,c_{e,v}+\sum_{v\ne v^\star}[\mathrm{logit}\,c_{e,v}]_+,\label{eq:fusion}
\end{equation}
with $v^\star$ the best view; weak blocked views cannot veto it, unlike naive-Bayes fusion.
\end{revised}

\section{Evaluation}\label{sec:eval}
\begin{revised}
\paragraph{Setup.} One library runs in a fast simulator and in ROS~2 Humble nodes flying PX4 x500
quadrotors in Gazebo Harmonic through Micro XRCE-DDS, with YOLO26n running on board, both dropping
packets by sampled SINR. Each run generates an area from its seed
(\nVictims{} victims, rubble, warm debris) and lasts \nDuration\,s; by default \nArea\,m
square, $N=\nNuav$, urban rubble, Poisson crashes (\nFailRate\,s$^{-1}$), UAVs at \nAlt\,m and
\nVmax\,m/s. In the measured runs the detector is a parameterised model of confidence versus range and
occlusion, with warm-debris false alarms. Baselines: B0 PSO; B1 our first
version; B2 PSO with an outage penalty; B3 exact-$\lambda_2$ control
\cite{sabattini2013}; B4 edge potentials \cite{ji2007}; B5 MAPPO \cite{yu2022mappo}; B6 sweep search. All were tuned with one Optuna budget on $J$
over separate seeds (SENTINEL: $T_h=\nTh$\,s, $\lambda_0=\nLamZero$, $\beta=\nBeta$), and MAPPO was trained to a plateau; all methods share seeds 0--\nSeedMax.

\paragraph{Results.} SENTINEL leads in all four channel profiles, significantly against every baseline
except in the severe one (open \nChOpenS{} vs.\ \nChOpenB, suburban \nChSubS{} vs.\ \nChSubB, urban
\nChUrbS{} vs.\ \nChUrbB, severe \nChSevS{} vs.\ \nChSevB). In urban rubble it
reports \nEthreeGainLo--\nEthreeGainHi\,\% more victims than the best baseline in every failure setting
(Table~\ref{tab:e3}). On the \nArea\,m area it beats every baseline
significantly from \nEoneNmin{} to \nEoneNmax{} UAVs (Table~\ref{tab:e1}); on a denser \nDenseArea\,m
area it leads at every size but significantly only at $N=\nEoneTwoHundredSigN$ (\nEoneDenseS{} vs.\
\nEoneDenseMappo{} for MAPPO).
\end{revised}

\paragraph{Where the Gains Come From.} Removing fusion (\nUrbNofus) or delivery (\nUrbNodel) lowers
the urban share significantly from \nUrbS{} (Table~\ref{tab:attr}); a mesh kept connected at all times
falls to \nStrictLo--\nStrictHi. With SENTINEL's fusion rule the best urban baseline rises from
\nUrbBestOrig{} to \nUrbBestFus{} (\nUrbBestFusName) yet all seven stay significantly behind, while in a
dense \nDenseN-UAV swarm baselines with fusion reach \nDenseFusLo--\nDenseFusHi{} and \nDenseSigAbove{}
leads significantly. With eight UAVs, $T_h=\nThUrbBest$\,s raises the share over never delivering from
\nThUrbInf{} to \nThUrbBestV{} (urban) and \nThSevInf{} to \nThSevBestV{} (severe); with four UAVs the effect is not significant.
\end{revised}
\begin{revised}
\paragraph{Link Preservation.} Over all experiments, reliable preserved links failed within a step \nViolAll{} times in \nStepsAll{} link-steps ($\nViolRate$). With the projection off on
the same \nSafetySettings{} settings and seeds, violations rose from \nViolOn{} in \nStepsOn{} link-steps
(in \nRunsOn{} of \nRunsTotal{} runs) to \nViolOff{} in \nStepsOff{} (in \nRunsOff{} of \nRunsTotal{}
runs), a \nViolFold-fold difference ($p<\nViolP$); the on-time share does not change.

\paragraph{PX4/Gazebo and the First Scenario.} \nEightN{} PX4 UAVs flew SENTINEL and B0 on
\nEightWorlds{} \nEightArea\,m worlds with one crash; reported victims, packet delivery and connected
time agree with the fast simulator within \nEightMaxDiff; B0 is slightly ahead in both
(\nEightSitlB{} vs.\ \nEightSitlS{} in Gazebo).
In the \nNineArea\,m, \nNineN-UAV scenario of the first version all methods stay connected; confirmed
victims are \nNineS{} for SENTINEL, \nNineBzero{} for B0 (not significantly fewer) and \nNineBone{} for
the first version (significantly fewer). Its earlier retention and discovery figures are withdrawn.

\paragraph{Detector.} On HIT-UAV \cite{hituav}, YOLO26n \cite{yolo26} matches YOLOv8n in validation
mAP50 (\nYnMap{} vs.\ \nVeMap) with \nYnParamRed\,\% fewer parameters and \nYnFlopRed\,\% fewer
operations, at \nYnTrt{} vs.\ \nVeTrt\,ms TensorRT latency on a desktop RTX~3060 (embedded latency
not measured).
\end{revised}

\section{Limitations and \kept{Conclusion}}
\begin{revised}
Results are simulated, with published channel ranges and no flights; detector accuracy is a HIT-UAV
validation result from above our \nAlt\,m, and the loop uses a hand-parameterised detector model. Victims within the \nGate\,m association gate
can merge, MAPPO used one reward design, and latency was measured on a desktop GPU only. SENTINEL reported more victims within 60\,s
than seven tuned baselines in most settings and was never significantly worse than any baseline in its
original form. Fusion and information-triggered delivery produced the gains (in dense swarms fusion
alone); the outage spring and link preservation are a safety property, cutting link violations
\nViolFold-fold.
\end{revised}
