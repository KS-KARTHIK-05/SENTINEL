\begin{abstract}
\begin{revised}
UAV swarms searching a disaster area without infrastructure must relay victim reports to
responders over their own links. SENTINEL treats the network as an elastic mesh that
stretches and breaks during search and is re-formed only to deliver a confirmed report: Elastic-Mesh
PSO (EM-PSO) plans waypoints, confirmed victims trigger delivery, weak detections get a
second view, and an outage-based Signal-Spring keeps delivery links reliable. In about
\nMissions{} simulated missions with a \nFreq\,GHz outage channel, \nEoneNmin--\nEoneNmax{} UAVs, six
failure settings and \nBaseWord{} tuned baselines, SENTINEL reports \nEthreeGainLo--\nEthreeGainHi\,\% more
victims within 60\,s than the best baseline in urban rubble (\nRobGainLo--\nRobGainHi\,\% with a
detector model fitted to rendered-frame detector outputs), and an always-connected mesh reports far
fewer in time.
\end{revised}
\end{abstract}

\section{\kept{Introduction}}
\begin{revised}
After an earthquake, thermal-camera UAVs can detect people in rubble on board \cite{hituav,yolo26},
but a detection matters only once responders receive it over short-range links that buildings block,
fading weakens and crashes cut. A connected swarm delivers at once but searches little, a spread-out
one finds more but delivers by chance; we measure this trade-off as the share of victims reported
within 60\,s.

Our first version held each UAV within 50\,m of its nearest neighbour, but a fixed radius ignores
rubble, a close neighbour does not imply a connected network (Lemma~\ref{lem:nn}), and its 25\,m test
area was smaller than the radio range. This revision contributes (i)~\emph{information-triggered
delivery}: a UAV stops searching when a victim it confirmed has not reached the responder within a hold
time $T_h$; (ii)~\emph{corroborative sensing}: weak detections get a lateral second view, fused so that
a blocked angle cannot veto a clear one; and (iii)~\emph{EM-PSO with an outage-based Signal-Spring and
link preservation}: EM-PSO plans the waypoints throughout, while its spring and link preservation act
only during delivery, as a safety property that keeps preserved links reliable
(Proposition~\ref{prop:link}) and cuts link violations \nViolFold-fold; the reporting gains come from
(i) and (ii). Code, data and extended tables: \url{https://github.com/KS-KARTHIK-05/SENTINEL} (branch v2).
\end{revised}

\section{\kept{Related Work}}
\begin{revised}
Connectivity controllers keep the graph connected through its algebraic connectivity
$\lambda_2$ \cite{zavlanos2008,sabattini2013} or edge potentials \cite{ji2007}, assuming a disk radio model. Intermittent connectivity schedules meetings
\cite{kantaros2017}, and age-of-information collection serves known sources
\cite{bafarassat2026aoi}, whereas victims are found by the search itself. Distributed PSO and Bayesian
search \cite{yang2024dapso,niu2026eventbayes} assume communication when needed, and MARL such as
MAPPO \cite{yu2022mappo} needs a trained policy. None combines
online-discovered sources, a deadline and an obstruction-aware channel.
\end{revised}

\section{System Model}\label{sec:model}
\begin{revised}
$N$ UAVs fly at altitude $h$ over tall no-fly, link-blocking buildings and low rubble that hides
victims; a responder radio at the launch point is the sink. Each slot $\Delta t$ every UAV
broadcasts its position, victim ledger and acknowledgements, received at $j$ with SINR
\begin{equation}
\gamma_{ij}=\frac{P_tG^2\,10^{-(\mathrm{PL}(d_{ij})+n_{ij}L_o+X_{ij})/10}\,|h_{ij}|^2}{\sigma^2+\sum_{k\in\mathcal{I}_j}\bar P_{kj}},\label{eq:sinr}
\end{equation}
with log-distance path loss $\mathrm{PL}(d)=\mathrm{PL}(d_0)+10\eta\log_{10}(d/d_0)$, $n_{ij}$
obstacles at $L_o$ each, correlated shadowing $X_{ij}$, Rician fading (Rayleigh when obstructed) and
colliding transmitters $\mathcal{I}_j$. Decoding needs $\gamma_{ij}\ge\gamma_{\mathrm{th}}$, so
\begin{equation}
P_{\mathrm{out}}(d,n)=\mathbb{E}_X\big[F_{|h|^2}\big(\gamma_{\mathrm{th}}/(\bar\gamma(d,n)\,10^{X/10})\big)\big],\label{eq:pout}
\end{equation}
and the reliable range is $R_\varepsilon(n)=\max\{d:P_{\mathrm{out}}(d,n)\le\varepsilon\}$. The link
budget ($\nFreq$\,GHz, $P_t=\nPtx$\,dBm, $G=\nGant$\,dBi, $B=\nBw$\,MHz, noise figure \nNF\,dB,
$\gamma_{\mathrm{th}}=\nGth$\,dB) leaves \nBudget\,dB of path loss before fading margins, and profiles
follow published ranges \cite{tr36777,itup1411}. In urban rubble a link is reliable to
\nRurbanZero\,m in the open and \nRurbanOne\,m behind one obstruction (Table~\ref{tab:channel}), so the
old 50\,m radius describes one obstructed link. With $\tau_k$ victim $k$'s first confirmation and
$T_k$ its delivery time ($\infty$ if never), the team maximises from local information
\begin{equation}
J=\frac{1}{|\mathcal{V}|}\sum_{k\in\mathcal{V}}e^{-T_k/\theta},\qquad\theta=\nTheta\,\mathrm{s}.\label{eq:J}
\end{equation}
\end{revised}

\section{SENTINEL}\label{sec:method}
\begin{revised}
\paragraph{Mode Switching.} With $\mathcal{E}_i(t)$ the confirmed, undelivered entries UAV $i$ owns
(its own, and any older than $3T_h$, so a crashed UAV's copies still arrive), it enters \textsc{Deliver} when
$\max_{e\in\mathcal{E}_i(t)}(t-\tau_e)\ge T_h$ and returns to \textsc{Explore} when $\mathcal{E}_i(t)$ is empty.

\paragraph{EM-PSO.} Each planning period a UAV picks the reachable waypoint that maximises
\begin{equation}
F_i(x)=w_c\hat G_i(x)+w_d\!\!\sum_{e\in\mathcal{T}_i}\!c_e e^{-\frac{\|x-x_e\|^2}{2s^2}}
+\frac{w_g(\|x_i-g_i\|-\|x-g_i\|)-w_t\|x-x_i\|}{vT_p}-S_i(x),\label{eq:fitness}
\end{equation}
with $\hat G_i$ expected new detections, $\mathcal{T}_i$ the tentative entries, $g_i$ a frontier
goal and $w_t$ a travel cost. The Signal-Spring acts only while tethered (delivering or relaying):
\begin{equation}
S_i(x)=\lambda_i\sum_{j\in\mathcal{S}_i}\Big(\frac{[P_{\mathrm{out}}(\|x-x_j\|,n(x,x_j))-\varepsilon_i]_+}{\varepsilon_i}\Big)^2,\label{eq:spring}
\end{equation}
where $\lambda_i=\lambda_0(1+\beta U_i)$, $\varepsilon_i=\varepsilon/(1+\beta U_i)$ and $U_i$ counts
entries only $i$ holds; it penalises outage, not distance. A constriction PSO
($\chi=\nPsoChi$, $c_1=c_2=\nPsoC$) \cite{clerc2002} maximises $F_i$.

\paragraph{Link Preservation.} $\mathcal{S}_i$ holds $i$'s neighbours in the local minimum spanning
tree of reliable links \cite{li2003lmst}.
\begin{lemma}\label{lem:nn}
Nearest-neighbour distance constraints do not imply connectivity.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Two UAV pairs 10\,m wide and 190\,m apart satisfy $R=50$\,m, yet no link joins them.
\end{proof}
Tethered UAV $i$ scales its step $u_i$ by the largest $\alpha\in[0,1]$ such that $x_i'=x_i+\alpha u_i$ meets
\begin{equation}
\|x_i'-m_{ij}\|\le\tfrac12 r_{ij}-\tfrac12 v\,a_{ij},\quad r_{ij}=R_\varepsilon\big(n^{\delta_{ij}}(x_i',\hat x_j)\big)\quad\forall j\in\mathcal{S}_i,\label{eq:proj}
\end{equation}
with $\hat x_j$ the last reported position of $j$ (age $a_{ij}$), $m_{ij}=(x_i+\hat x_j)/2$,
$\delta_{ij}=v(\Delta t+a_{ij})$ and $n^\delta$ counting obstacles inflated by $\delta$.
\begin{proposition}\label{prop:link}
If $j\in\mathcal{S}_i$, $i\in\mathcal{S}_j$, both UAVs are tethered, speeds are bounded by $v$,
positions are at most $a$ old and both steps satisfy Eq.~\eqref{eq:proj}, link $(i,j)$ is
$\varepsilon$-reliable at $t+\Delta t$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
Midpoint estimates err by $\le va/2$, so Eq.~\eqref{eq:proj} and the triangle inequality give
$\|x_i'-x_j'\|\le\frac12(r_{ij}+r_{ji})$; $R_\varepsilon$ is non-increasing in the obstacle count, which
the inflated count bounds.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Corroborative Sensing.} An entry with $\nClo\le c_e<\nChi$ requests the nearest neighbour's
view $\pm\nFlank$\,m across the line of sight, which at $h=\nAlt$\,m looks \nFlankAngle$^\circ$
off nadir past a slab edge at \nFlankSlant\,\% more slant range. With $c_{e,v}$ the best confidence
per sector $v$, SENTINEL fuses
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{logit}\,c_e=\max_v\mathrm{logit}\,c_{e,v}+\sum_{v\ne v^\star}[\mathrm{logit}\,c_{e,v}]_+,\label{eq:fusion}
\end{equation}
with $v^\star$ the best view; weak blocked views cannot veto it, unlike naive-Bayes fusion.
\end{revised}

\begin{revised}
\paragraph{Setup.} One library runs in a fast simulator and in ROS~2 Humble nodes flying PX4 x500
quadrotors in Gazebo Harmonic through Micro XRCE-DDS, with YOLO26n running on board, both dropping
packets by sampled SINR. Each run generates an area from its seed
(\nVictims{} victims, rubble, warm debris) and lasts \nDuration\,s; by default \nArea\,m
square, $N=\nNuav$, urban rubble, Poisson crashes (\nFailRate\,s$^{-1}$), UAVs at \nAlt\,m and
\nVmax\,m/s, and a hand-set detector model (confidence vs.\ range and occlusion; warm-debris false
alarms). Baselines B0--B8: PSO; our first version; PSO with an outage penalty; exact-$\lambda_2$
control \cite{sabattini2013}; edge potentials \cite{ji2007}; MAPPO \cite{yu2022mappo}; sweep search; a
hard 50\,m nearest-neighbour wall; a responder relay chain. All were tuned with one Optuna budget on $J$
over separate seeds (SENTINEL: $T_h=\nTh$\,s, $\lambda_0=\nLamZero$, $\beta=\nBeta$); MAPPO trained to a plateau. All share seeds 0--\nSeedMax.

\paragraph{Results.} SENTINEL leads in all four channel profiles, significantly against all
except in the severe one (open \nChOpenS{} vs.\ \nChOpenB, suburban \nChSubS{} vs.\ \nChSubB, urban
\nChUrbS{} vs.\ \nChUrbB, severe \nChSevS{} vs.\ \nChSevB). In urban rubble it
reports \nEthreeGainLo--\nEthreeGainHi\,\% more victims than the best baseline in every failure setting
(Table~\ref{tab:e3}). At \nArea\,m it beats every baseline
significantly from \nEoneNmin{} to \nEoneNmax{} UAVs (Table~\ref{tab:e1}); on a denser \nDenseArea\,m
area its mean is highest except at \nEoneTwoHundredNotTop, but at no size significantly above all
\nBaseWord{} ($N=\nDenseN$: \nEoneDenseS{} vs.\ \nEoneDenseBest, \nEoneDenseBestName).
\end{revised}
\begin{revised}
\paragraph{Where the Gains Come From.} Removing fusion (\nUrbNofus) or delivery (\nUrbNodel) lowers
the urban share significantly from \nUrbS{} (Table~\ref{tab:attr}); a mesh kept connected at all times
falls to \nStrictLo--\nStrictHi. With SENTINEL's fusion rule the best urban baseline rises from
\nUrbBestOrig{} to \nUrbBestFus{} (\nUrbBestFusName) yet all \nBaseWord{} stay significantly behind; in a
dense \nDenseN-UAV swarm baselines with fusion reach \nDenseFusLo--\nDenseFusHi{} and \nDenseSigAbove{}
leads significantly.
\end{revised}
\begin{revised}
\paragraph{Link Preservation.} Over all runs, reliable preserved links failed within a step \nViolAll{} times in \nStepsAll{} link-steps ($\nViolRate$). Without the projection
(same \nSafetySettings{} settings, seeds) violations rose from \nViolOn{} in \nStepsOn{} to
\nViolOff{} in \nStepsOff{} link-steps, a \nViolFold-fold difference ($p<\nViolP$), with the on-time
share unchanged. Varying $\lambda_0$ (\nSensLamLo--\nSensLamHi), $\beta$ (\nSensBetaLo--\nSensBetaHi)
or $\varepsilon$ (\nSensEpsLo--\nSensEpsHi), or a distance spring ($R_{\max}=\nSensRmaxLo$--\nSensRmaxHi\,m)
changes the share by $\le$\,\nSensMaxDelta, never significantly.

\paragraph{PX4/Gazebo and the First Scenario.} \nEightN{} PX4 UAVs flew SENTINEL and B0 on
\nEightWorlds{} \nEightArea\,m worlds with one crash; reported victims, packet delivery and connected
time agree with the fast simulator within \nEightMaxDiff; B0 is slightly ahead in both
(\nEightSitlB{} vs.\ \nEightSitlS{} in Gazebo). With the fine-tuned YOLO26n (below) in the loop on \nSixN{} such worlds,
SENTINEL reports \nSixS{} and B0 \nSixB{} (difference $\nSixDiff$, 95\,\% CI $\nSixDiffLo$ to
$\nSixDiffHi$, $p=\nSixP$; fast simulator \nSixFastS{} vs.\ \nSixFastB).
In the first version's \nNineArea\,m, \nNineN-UAV scenario all methods stay connected; confirmed
victims are \nNineS{} for SENTINEL, \nNineBzero{} for B0 (not significantly fewer) and \nNineBone{} for
the first version (significantly fewer); its retention and discovery figures are withdrawn.

\paragraph{Detector.} On HIT-UAV \cite{hituav}, YOLO26n \cite{yolo26} matches YOLOv8n in validation
mAP50 (\nYnMap{} vs.\ \nVeMap) with \nYnParamRed\,\% fewer parameters and \nYnFlopRed\,\% fewer
operations (TensorRT \nYnTrt{} vs.\ \nVeTrt\,ms on a desktop RTX~3060).
On Gazebo-rendered thermal frames at 25--30\,m (not real footage) it reaches \nRdHMap{} mAP50 on
\nRdTestWorlds{} unseen worlds, and \nRdFMap{} after fine-tuning on \nRdTrainWorlds{} others, with recall
\nRdFRecLoOpen{} (open) and \nRdFRecLoOcc{} (partly occluded) at confidence 0.40 and \nRdFFaLo{} false
alarms per frame on warm debris. With the in-loop model fitted to these outputs (no re-tuning),
SENTINEL keeps the highest mean in the six urban settings of Table~\ref{tab:e3}, but leads the best
baseline, \nRobBestName, by only \nRobGainLo--\nRobGainHi\,\%, never significantly.
\end{revised}

\section{Limitations and \kept{Conclusion}}
\begin{revised}
Results are simulated with published channel ranges and no flights; detector accuracy at 25--30\,m is
measured on rendered frames only, and the loop uses a detector model hand-set or fitted to detector
outputs on rendered frames. Victims within the \nGate\,m association gate
can merge, MAPPO used one reward design, and no embedded latency was measured. SENTINEL reported more victims within 60\,s
than \nBaseWord{} tuned baselines in most settings (with the fitted detector model by
\nRobGainLo--\nRobGainHi\,\%, not significantly) and was never significantly worse
than any baseline in its original form. Fusion and information-triggered delivery produced the gains (in dense swarms fusion
alone); the outage spring and link preservation are a safety property, cutting violations
\nViolFold-fold.
\end{revised}
